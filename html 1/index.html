Every skill has value. Every person has something to offer.

Many people have useful skills but struggle to find clients or earn from them. Meanwhile, students, individuals, and small businesses need affordable help and don’t know where to find capable people nearby.

SkillSalo connects these two sides. You don’t need to be a professional freelancer: if you can apply makeup, fix a chair, edit a video, or tutor mathematics, someone near you can use that.

Our prototype

SkillSalo is made of three parts that work together. Scroll sideways to see the screens.

Seven features

  1. AI QuickMatch

    Describe what you need. We rank suitable providers by skill, budget, location, and availability.

  2. OpenMatch

    Seekers post the services they need. Providers post what they offer. Both receive inquiries.

  3. SkillBridge

    Pay for a service, swap skills directly, or mix both with a cash top-up. The agreement is recorded.

  4. Value Completion Record

    Tracks the agreed work, deliverables, payment or exchange status, and the final outcome.

  5. SkillProof

    Build credibility from completed work, ratings, reviews, and portfolio evidence. Not an official certification.

  6. SaloSquad

    Form a team with complementary skills for bigger projects and track each person’s contribution.

  7. SaloBoost

    Providers can promote a service or portfolio project through clearly labeled sponsored placements.

How a transaction works

  1. Open SkillSalo and discover skills
  2. Use AI QuickMatch or OpenMatch to view suitable providers
  3. Send an inquiry and negotiate
  4. Confirm a SkillBridge agreement
  5. Choose paid service, skill exchange, or hybrid
  6. Track the work and payment, then confirm completion
  7. Earn an eligible SkillProof record

About us

Our background

SkillSalo started as a startup project for our Entrepreneurship course at Davao del Norte State College. We noticed that small skills often go unused because the people who need them can’t find them, and the people who have them can’t reach customers.

Mission

To make small, practical, technical, and creative skills more accessible by helping people discover services, find opportunities, exchange skills, collaborate, and build credibility through completed work.

Vision

A community where every skill, however small, can become a useful service or opportunity.
